\section{Recorded replay and evidence boundaries}
Recorded analysis opens through the exact \texttt{.ssdecoder} and Descriptor. Source logs and decoder packages remain immutable. Explicit Offline What-if parameters must be labeled separately from recorded configuration; absent noise, initial state, validity or timing fields cannot be silently filled and called recorded.

Mathematical equivalence is distinct from numerical replay fidelity. Pure INS/KF6 currently lack an exact-validation reference on the ordinary reconstruction path; SF6 revision 2 is approximate when reconstructed, with unavailable status for missing required input. ESKF's recorded-parity path additionally checks actual Body inputs, complete initial state/covariance, timing, identities, measurement outcomes and state/covariance comparisons. A complete declared comparison can establish its recorded-data fidelity scope; it cannot establish real sensor truth or target hardware correctness. Missing covariance parts cannot be reported as compared successfully.

Host tests, source-graph checks, Power-of-Ten project gates, analyzer compilation and ARM linking establish only their executed revisions/configurations/scopes. Historical reports retain their original source identity, failed/skipped details and hashes. An unavailable hardware test is not a pass; unresolved critical contracts remain NOT PROVEN. This mathematical reference adds no new MCU WCET, stack, electrical, hardware/flight or USB acceptance evidence.
